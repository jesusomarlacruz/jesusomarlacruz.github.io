## Wireless Networks on SoCs Group

The Wireless Networks on SoCs Group, within the Wireless Networking Group at [IMDEA Networks Institute](https://networks.imdea.org/) (Madrid, Spain), is led by Jesus Omar Lacruz.

### Vision

The group builds the hardware architectures and real-time experimentation platforms that let wireless and sensing research run at the speeds and bandwidths real systems need -- from FPGA/RFSoC signal-processing pipelines to full mmWave and sub-6 GHz MIMO testbeds ([MIMORPH](/platforms/2_mimorph/), [HELIX](/platforms/1_helix/), [mm-FLEX](/platforms/3_mmflex/); see the [Platforms](/platforms/) page for all of them). Current work extends this into integrated sensing and communication (ISAC) and the hardware foundations of 6G, with the team's research spanning near-field communication, System-on-Chip and wireless platform design, and low-power and neuromorphic signal processing.
