mm-FLEX is an open platform for millimeter-wave mobile, full-bandwidth experimentation, presented at ACM MobiSys 2020 (Best Paper Award runner-up). It is one of the earlier platforms developed by the group to support mobile, full-bandwidth mm-wave research.
