MISO is a mixed hardware/software design for mm-wave experimentation (IEEE 802.11ad preamble) on software-defined radios, built on USRP devices with GNU Radio/RFNoC and running at a reduced bandwidth relative to the 1.76 GSPS standard rate. Released under the MIT license.
