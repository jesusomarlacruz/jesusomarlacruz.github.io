Jesus Omar Lacruz is a Senior Researcher in the Wireless Networking Group at IMDEA Networks Institute (Madrid, Spain). He leads the group’s Wireless Networks on SoCs sub-group, working on high-speed signal processing architectures and FPGA/RFSoC implementations for sub-6 GHz and millimeter-wave MIMO systems, and on integrated sensing and communication (ISAC) and 6G. He holds a Ph.D. in Electronic Engineering (Cum Laude) from the Universitat Politècnica de València, where he worked on low-complexity algorithms and hardware architectures for Non-Binary LDPC decoders. Before joining IMDEA Networks in 2017, he was a full-time professor at Universidad de Los Andes (Mérida, Venezuela) from 2010 to 2017.

The group’s platform and tool repositories are at github.com/IMDEANetworksWNG; see the Platforms page on this site for details.
